Zihuan Qiu (邱子欢)

I am currently a third-year Ph.D. student at the School of Information and Communication Engineering, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), supervised by Prof. Fanman Meng. I received my M.S. from UESTC in 2023.

My research primarily centers on three directions. (1) Continual learning of models: enabling neural networks to continuously acquire new knowledge without forgetting previously learned abilities, with a focus on stability-plasticity trade-offs and data-free replay strategies. (2) Model parameter merging: developing efficient techniques to integrate multiple task-specific models into a single unified model that preserves multi-task performance, including null-space filtering and mixture-of-experts approaches. (3) Agent self-evolution: equipping autonomous LLM and VLM agents with long-term memory and tool-use capabilities to tackle open-ended workflows through recursive skill learning and environment-driven adaptation.
